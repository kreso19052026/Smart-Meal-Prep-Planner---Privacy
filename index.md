Smart Meal Planner ("the app") is developed by Kozo Technology. This policy
explains what data the app handles and the choices you have.
Contact: kozo.technology@gmail.com

## Data stored on your device
Your meal plans, recipes (including photos you add to your own recipes),
shopping lists and settings are stored only on your device, in a private
database. No account is needed, and the app does not send this data to us
or to anyone else. Recipe exports are shared only where you choose to share
them. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## Advertising and your consent
This app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's
advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device and
operating-system information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure
ads. See how Google uses this information:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland:** on first launch the app asks
for your consent through Google's certified consent management platform.
Personalised ads are shown only if you consent; otherwise you may see limited,
non-personalised ads.

**United States:** if you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law
(such as California), you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal
information for targeted advertising.

**Change your choice at any time:** open the app's **Settings → Privacy
options**.

## Fonts
The app downloads its fonts from Google Fonts the first time they are needed.
Google receives your IP address for that request:
https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect data from them.

## Changes to this policy
When this policy changes, we update it on this page and change the
"Last updated" date above.
